StudyCafe

Find a study spot off campus.

A map-first study-spot finder for Western students who can't find a seat in Weldon. It pulls cafés from OpenStreetMap around your location or any area of the map, sorts them nearest first, and filters them by what matters for studying: quiet, Wi-Fi, outlets, open late and room for a group.

Filtering runs entirely in the browser and personal notes are stored on your device. Requests to the free public APIs are limited by cooldowns, caching and timeouts.

  • StackVanilla JavaScript, Leaflet, Overpass API
  • Built forAccessibility, privacy, no build step
  • NextExam-season mode, community study reports
